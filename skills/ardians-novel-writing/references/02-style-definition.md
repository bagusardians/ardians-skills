# Phase 2: Style Definition

Establish the target voice before any drafting. This phase composes
`technical-writing` for prose clarity and `brainstorming` to weigh options.

## Decisions to lock

- Point of view: first person, third limited, third omniscient, or multiple.
- Tense: past or present.
- Narrative distance: intimate, close, or detached.
- Syntax: typical sentence length and how much it varies.
- Diction: register (plain, literary, genre), vocabulary level, and forbidden words.
- Rhythm and pacing: scene versus summary ratio, chapter length.
- Tone: the emotional register the reader should feel.
- Imagery density: how often metaphor and sensory detail appear.

## Steps

1. Brainstorm two or three candidate voices against the premise.
2. Choose one and write the style guide as short, testable house rules.
3. Draft two or three sample paragraphs to the spec so the voice is concrete.
4. State explicitly what the voice must never do.

## Deliverable

A style guide containing the locked decisions, the house rules, and the sample
paragraphs.

## Gate

Present the style guide and sample paragraphs. Ask the user to approve or revise
before phase 3.

## Checklist

- Every decision above has an answer.
- House rules are testable, not adjectives.
- At least two sample paragraphs exist.
- Forbidden patterns are listed.
