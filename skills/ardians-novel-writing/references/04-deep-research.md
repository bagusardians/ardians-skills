# Phase 4: Deep Research

Factually ground the setting, topic, and period. This phase composes
`research-brief`, `evidence-based-citations`, `notion`, and `jupyter`.

## Steps

1. List the research questions the story depends on: place, period, technology,
   institutions, language, and daily life.
2. Research each with `research-brief`. Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
3. Support every factual claim with an exact quote and an official source using
   `evidence-based-citations`.
4. For data-heavy material, such as timelines, geography, or economics, analyze it in
   `jupyter` so the numbers in the story are consistent.
5. Optionally store the brief in `notion` for the user.
6. Mark clearly which details are real, which are invented, and which are uncertain.

## Deliverable

A research brief with sourced facts, an open-questions list, and a real-versus-invented
register.

## Gate

Present the brief and the open questions. Ask the user to approve before phase 5.

## Checklist

- Every factual claim has a source.
- Real, invented, and uncertain details are labeled.
- Open questions are listed for the author to resolve.
