# Phase 5: Genre Immersion

Study the genre so the book meets reader expectations while still having its own voice.
This phase composes `research-brief` and `use-jev`.

## Steps

1. Identify the primary genre and any secondary genres or subgenres.
2. Catalog conventions: the beats readers expect and where they must land.
3. List tropes to use, subvert, or avoid.
4. Note pacing norms: typical length, act structure, and where tension peaks.
5. Collect comparable titles and what they do well.
6. State content warnings and audience expectations.
7. Use `use-jev` to classify or score convention adherence where a judgment is needed.

## Deliverable

A genre playbook: conventions, tropes, pacing norms, comparables, content warnings, and
the specific ways this book will meet or deliberately break each convention.

## Gate

Present the playbook and confirm the conventions to honor and the ones to break before
phase 6.

## Checklist

- Expected beats are listed with target positions.
- Tropes are sorted into use, subvert, and avoid.
- Comparables are named.
- Content warnings are stated.
