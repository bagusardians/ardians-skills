# Phase 7: Editorial and Quality

Turn the draft into a clean manuscript. This phase composes `use-jev`,
`evidence-based-citations`, and `technical-writing`.

## Edit passes, in order

1. **Structural edit:** plot logic, character arcs, pacing, chapter order, and whether
   each scene earns its place.
2. **Line edit:** prose at the sentence level, voice consistency against the style guide,
   repetition, and clarity.
3. **Copy edit:** grammar, punctuation, spelling, and house style.
4. **Continuity:** names, dates, physical details, and timeline against the story bible.
5. **Sensitivity and representation:** check portrayals for stereotypes and harm; flag
   issues for the author rather than silently rewriting.
6. **Fact check:** verify real-world claims with `evidence-based-citations`.
7. **Quality scoring:** use `use-jev` to score dimensions such as pacing, character, and
   prose on a defined rubric.

## Deliverable

An edited manuscript plus an edit report listing changes made, unresolved issues, and
quality scores.

## Gate

Present the edit report and the edited draft. Ask the user to approve before phase 8.

## Checklist

- Every pass ran, in order.
- Unresolved issues are listed, not hidden.
- Quality scores use a stated rubric.
